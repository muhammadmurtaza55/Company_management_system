Batch Monthly Payroll Generation

Automatically generate monthly salary records for all active employees based on their structural basic salary.

How Batch Generation Works:

The system scans all active employees in the selected target department (or company-wide), pulls each employee's configured basic monthly salary, initializes bonuses and deductions to $0.00, sets Net Salary = Basic Salary, and registers the disbursement record.
